Hemisphere

Cortical territories come from the Yeo 2011 seven-network map (liberal mask), sampled at every vertex. The salience network is Yeo's “ventral attention” network and the central executive is the “frontoparietal control” network.

Subcortical nodes are lit by convention, not from the Yeo map: salience → amygdala, nucleus accumbens, substantia nigra, ventral tegmental area, hypothalamus, periaqueductal grey (Seeley 2007); default mode → hippocampus (Buckner 2008).

Pathways

Pathways are schematic: animated lines join the atlas centroids of each relay to show direction of flow. They are not tractography; switch on the White matter tracts group for real fibre bundles.

Receptor map

Group-average PET and SPECT maps from healthy adults, compiled by Hansen et al. 2022 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Each map is scaled to its own brain range, so colours compare regions within a map, not receptors with each other.

Drugs

Picking a drug shows the map of its main mapped target; tap its other targets in the card to switch. Affinity dots (●●● highest) are approximate teaching tiers from standard binding data, not measured values.

Data
Cortex, deep grey matter, ventricles: Hammers 83-region atlas (Hammers 2003; Gousias 2008). Hypothalamus, habenula, mammillary bodies, subthalamic nucleus, ventral pallidum, red nucleus, ventral tegmental area: CIT168 (Pauli 2018). Thalamic nuclei and mammillothalamic tract: THOMAS (Su 2019). Basal forebrain: Julich-Brain 3. Fourth ventricle: CerebrA. Raphe, locus coeruleus, periaqueductal grey: centroids from the Brainstem Navigator, shapes schematic. Tracts: HCP842 tractography atlas (Yeh 2018). Networks: Yeo 2011. MRI: MNI152 (FSL, 1 mm). All in MNI152 6th-generation space; 2009-space atlases moved with the neuroparc ANTs transform. Atlas files via neuroparc and Lead-DBS.
